# Influencer × Brand Collab

A two-sided marketplace that connects **influencers** and **brands** for paid collaborations.

Every profile is verified by AI, scored for trust, and badged before either side can chat, match, or close a deal. An AI deal agent handles negotiation, contracts, and handoff—so both sides can skip the cold DMs and spreadsheet chaos.

## What it does

- **AI-verified profiles** — Review profile signals and verify identity, audience quality, and brand or creator information.
- **Trust scores and badges** — Make informed decisions with clear, comparable credibility signals.
- **Two-sided discovery** — Help brands find relevant creators and influencers find campaigns that fit their audience.
- **Deal agent** — Support negotiation, contract generation, approvals, and handoff in one workflow.
- **Safer collaboration** — Keep matching and deal-making behind verification so both sides can work with more confidence.
